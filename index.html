FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What is Quora marketing?

Quora marketing uses relevant questions and useful answers to increase brand awareness, educate potential customers and create discoverable content around topics related to a business.

Do you provide Threads marketing?

Yes. Converza provides Threads campaign planning, sponsored content coordination and conversation-focused marketing campaigns.

Do you offer X marketing services?

Yes. Campaigns can include sponsored posts, creator collaborations and relevant conversation campaigns designed around a brand's objectives.

Can you manage bulk campaigns?

Yes. We can coordinate campaigns across multiple platforms, markets and campaign requirements.

Do you guarantee followers or sales?

No. Marketing performance depends on the product, audience, creative, offer, platform and other factors. Campaign deliverables and measurable activity are defined before work begins.

Do you use fake engagement?

Our positioning is based on legitimate marketing, sponsored placements and meaningful participation. Campaigns should follow the applicable platform rules and disclosure requirements.
